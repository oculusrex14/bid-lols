# 00_PRODUCT.md — Bidthrone: Internet Bounty Network

**Status:** Active product definition for the bounty-network pivot. The old pay-to-rank model is discontinued; the legacy audit lives in `docs/LEGACY_MIGRATION.md`. This document is the living product definition and supersedes the stale `SPEC.md` and in-app `/spec` for product intent.

## Umbrella

**Bidthrone** is an internet bounty network.

Core idea: **"Put money on a problem. See who takes the throne."**

Sponsors put money on a problem; qualified participants compete to solve it; the sponsor verifies and selects; completed work is paid out and credited as reputation.

| Domain | Product |
|---|---|
| `foundersbid.lol` | Startup execution marketplace |
| `culturebid.lol` | Creative bounty marketplace |
| `bidception.lol` | Nested / team bounties (future) |
| `bidthrone.lol` | Reputation & discovery umbrella |

## FoundersBid.lol — Startup Execution Marketplace

Two primary modes:

- **Bounty** — multiple qualified participants may compete and submit work; the sponsor selects winner(s).
- **Project** — providers submit proposals before doing the work; the sponsor chooses one provider; work then proceeds using defined milestones.

Candidate initial use cases: development, AI automation, design, product, research, data work, marketing/GTM, content, business operations.

**Future feature, NOT Phase 00:** Startup Graveyard for abandoned startups/repos/domains.

## CultureBid.lol — Creative Bounty Marketplace

A marketplace for creative bounties. Candidate categories: UGC, memes, video, photography, design, writing, naming, social content, music, brand challenges.

Constraint: future cultural/entertainment mechanics must not become core infrastructure.

## Bidception.lol — Nested Bounties (Future)

A parent project/bounty can be decomposed by a **captain** into smaller, funded child bounties. This requires advanced accounting and is NOT to be implemented early.

## Bidthrone.lol — Reputation & Discovery

Umbrella reputation and discovery product. Ranks people based on genuine completed work and outcomes. Users cannot simply pay to rank.

Candidate future leaderboards: Top Builders, Top Creators, Top Captains, Rising, Most Reliable, Highest Rated.

The Crown visual language can eventually represent earned reputation. (The legacy Crown prediction product is REMOVE; only the visual identity is in scope here.)

## Product Principles

- Genuine utility before entertainment.
- No fake activity.
- No fake scarcity.
- No artificial traffic scaling.
- No pay-to-rank reputation.
- No casino/gambling-style paid mechanics.
- Do not optimize for speculative virality at the expense of utility.
- Participants should understand how rewards work.
- Avoid exploitative speculative work.
- Support bounded participant counts and qualification where relevant.
- Support winner-take-all, podium, and finalist-pool payout structures (eventually).
- Platform fees are transparent: a bounty reward presented to participants must not be silently reduced by hidden platform commissions.
- Design for India initially, retaining global extensibility.

## Monetization Direction

Candidate models — directional, NOT Phase 00 implementation requirements:

- Sponsor/platform fee on funded bounties
- Managed bounty premium
- Provider subscriptions
- Featured opportunities
- Employer/company plans
- Successful acquisition fee in Graveyard
- Managed nested project fees
